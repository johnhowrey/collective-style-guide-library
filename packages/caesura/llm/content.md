# Caesura — Content & Microcopy Guide

> Editorial register. Every word load-bearing.

## Voice tenets

1. **Sentences earn their length.** Short for emphasis; long when nuance requires.
2. **Active voice by default.** Passive only when the agent is genuinely unknown.
3. **Specific over generic.** "The 1962 edition" beats "an early edition".
4. **Curly quotes, em dashes, ellipses.** Real ones (… not ...).

## Patterns

### Buttons
- Sentence case, often a single verb. "Save", "Continue", "Print"
- Reference object when ambiguous: "Send for review"

### Headings
- Title case for major headings. "On the Etymology of the Em Dash"
- Sentence case for sub-sections

### Errors
- Apologetic, brief. "We couldn't reach the server. Trying again."

### Empty states
- Editorial framing. "Nothing yet — make the first entry."

### Notifications
- Whisper. "Saved." "Sent." "Done."

## Do / Don't

| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| "Click here for more!" | "Read more →" |
| "Oh no!! Page not found." | "Page not found." |
| "Thanks 4 reading!" | "Thank you for reading." |
| "Submit" | "Send" / "Save" / "Publish" |

## For LLMs

1. Always use curly quotes, em dashes, and proper ellipses
2. Avoid contractions in formal copy ("do not" over "don't" in headings)
3. No exclamation in error or notification copy
4. Title case for major editorial headings; sentence case otherwise
