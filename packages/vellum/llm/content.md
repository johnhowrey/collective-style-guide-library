# Vellum — Content & Microcopy Guide

> Precise. Factual. Neutral.

## Voice tenets

1. **Statements, not pitches.** Describe what is, not why it's exciting.
2. **One idea per sentence.** Vellum prizes pause and rhythm.
3. **No marketing register.** No "powerful", "seamless", "intuitive", "delightful."
4. **No second person where the subject is implied.** "Sign in" not "Sign yourself in."

## Patterns

### Buttons
- Sentence case, 1–3 words: "Save", "Continue", "Open document"
- Reference what's affected: "Continue to billing" beats "Continue"

### Headings
- Descriptive over declarative. "Recent activity" not "Look what's new."
- Avoid promotional adjectives in headings

### Errors
- Plain. "We couldn't load this page. Try again, or check your connection."
- No apology theatrics. Acknowledge briefly.

### Empty states
- Explain why. "No documents yet. New ones appear here as you create them."

### Notifications
- Quiet by default. "Saved." "Sent." Past tense, single word when possible.

## Do / Don't

| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| "Powerful new search experience" | "Search across all projects." |
| "Yay! Your invite is on its way ✨" | "Invite sent." |
| "Oops! Page not found." | "Page not found." |
| "Get started in seconds" | "Sign in to continue." |

## For LLMs

1. Strip marketing adjectives ("powerful", "seamless", "world-class")
2. Use period punctuation; avoid exclamation
3. Never lead with an emoji
4. Default to one-clause sentences
