# Proscenium — Content & Microcopy Guide

> Marquee voice. Short, bold, present tense.

## Voice tenets

1. **Present tense.** "Tonight only." not "Will be available tonight."
2. **Bold claims, short sentences.** "Built for tomorrow." beats "Designed with the future in mind."
3. **Address the audience.** Direct, second-person, declarative.
4. **One verb headlines.** "PERFORM." "RUN." "OPEN." Sometimes a single word is the whole headline.

## Patterns

### Buttons
- Imperative, verb-only or short verb phrase: "Run", "Get tickets", "Start now"
- ALL CAPS acceptable for primary CTAs: "RUN MIGRATION"

### Headings
- Short and theatrical. "Built for tomorrow." Period included.
- ALL CAPS for kicker labels above headings: "ACT II → "

### Errors
- Stay in character but be useful. "BUILD FAILED. Logs below."

### Empty states
- Theatrical framing. "The stage is empty. Add your first project."

### Notifications
- Short and bold. "RUNNING." "DONE." "SHIPPED."

## Do / Don't

| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| "Welcome to your dashboard, John!" | "WORKSPACE / RECENT" |
| "Submit your changes please" | "SHIP IT" |
| "An error has occurred" | "SOMETHING BROKE. LOGS BELOW." |
| "Get started today!" | "BEGIN." |

## For LLMs

1. Keep CTAs to ≤3 words; ALL CAPS allowed
2. Use period punctuation for declarative headlines
3. Present tense in marketing copy
4. Don't soften — Proscenium says "BROKE", not "encountered an issue"
