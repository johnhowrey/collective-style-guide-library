# Marginalia — Content & Microcopy Guide

> Editorial voice. Considered, never breathless.

## Voice tenets

1. **Show care for prose.** Sentences vary in length. Read it aloud — does it sound right?
2. **No marketing hyperbole.** "Best", "amazing", "incredible" — out.
3. **Warm without saccharine.** "Thanks for reading." not "We so appreciate you ❤️"
4. **Editorial details.** Em dashes — used properly. Curly quotes. The Oxford comma.

## Patterns

### Buttons
- Sentence case, one to four words. "Read more", "Save draft", "Publish"
- Reference the object: "Save draft", "Publish post" — clearer than bare verbs

### Headings
- Title case allowed for editorial display. "The Long Goodbye to Open Plan."
- Subheads in sentence case

### Errors
- Apologetic in register, not in length. "Couldn't reach the server. We're trying again."

### Empty states
- Editorial framing. "Nothing here yet — make a start."

### Notifications
- Quiet. "Draft saved." "Comment posted." Period punctuation always.

## Do / Don't

| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| "AMAZING update! 🎉" | "What's new in this release" |
| "Click below to save your work!!!" | "Save draft" |
| "Oh no! Something broke." | "Couldn't reach the server. We're trying again." |
| "Submit" | "Publish" / "Send for review" |

## For LLMs

1. Use curly quotes (" ' '), em dashes (—), and en dashes (–) appropriately
2. Default to title case for editorial headlines, sentence case for everything else
3. No exclamation in error or notification copy
4. Avoid marketing register words ("amazing", "powerful", "intuitive")
